      About the numbers

      MET values come from the 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities (Herrmann et al., 2024; pacompendium.com) — activities plus quick presets that point at Compendium codes.

      Calories are estimates. Rep-based moves convert reps to time using a seconds-per-rep you can adjust. Runs, walks, rides and swims pick the Compendium MET that matches your speed.
